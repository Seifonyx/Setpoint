Today

Setpoint is free. There are no purchases or subscriptions, so nothing can be charged and there's nothing to refund.

When paid features arrive

This policy will apply to any purchase made in Setpoint:

  • 14-day refund. If you're not happy with a purchase, ask for a full refund within 14 days of buying it. No reason needed.
  • Subscriptions. You can cancel any time. You keep access until the end of the period you paid for, and you won't be charged again.
  • Problems. If a paid feature doesn't work as described and we can't fix it, we'll refund you, even after 14 days.
  • App stores. If you bought through the Apple App Store or Google Play, refunds go through them, under their rules; we'll help where we can.

To ask for a refund, email setpointapp.support@gmail.com with the email address on your account and the date of purchase. Refunds go back to the original payment method, normally within 14 days. This doesn't affect your rights under Egypt's Consumer Protection Law (Law No. 181 of 2018) or the law where you live.

Questions? Email setpointapp.support@gmail.com.
