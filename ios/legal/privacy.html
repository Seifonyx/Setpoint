Privacy Policy

Setpoint for iPhone · Effective 18 November 2026 · Setpoint, Egypt

Who we are

Setpoint is a food, nutrition and weight tracking app for iPhone. It is run by an individual based in Egypt under the name Setpoint ("we", "us"). We are the controller of the personal data described here.

Contact for anything about your data: setpointapp.support@gmail.com.

The short version

  • Setpoint needs an account. What you log is kept on your iPhone and in your account, so it's safe if you change phones. We use it only to run the app for you.
  • Photo recognition, label reading, and barcode scanning all happen on your iPhone. Your photos aren't sent anywhere to be analysed.
  • If you speak what you ate, Apple's speech recognition turns it into text, on your iPhone where it can.
  • We don't show ads, don't use analytics or tracking, and don't sell or rent your data.
  • You can download everything (You → Back up, or You → Export to a spreadsheet) and delete your account and its data at any time (You → Account → Delete account).

What we collect and why

1. Your account. Your email address and an account ID. If you sign in with Google, Google tells us your email address (and shares your name and profile picture with the sign-in service); Setpoint keeps only your email address and account ID. Passwords are handled by Google Firebase Authentication; we never see or store your password.

2. What you enter in the app. Your profile (sex, birth year, height and, if you add it, body fat), weigh-ins, food diary, your own foods, recipes and meals, water, fasting, body measurements, progress photos, notes, targets and settings. This is used to show your diary, targets, trends and insights, and to keep your data in your account.

3. Health data. Weight, food intake, body measurements and photos are health data, which the law treats as sensitive. We store it in your account only with your explicit consent, which you give when you create an account or sign in. You can withdraw that consent at any time by deleting your account.

4. Apple Health. Only if you turn it on (You → Settings → Apple Health): Setpoint reads your weight and steps from Apple Health to fill in your weigh-ins and estimate what you burn, and, if you choose "Save weigh-ins to Apple Health", saves your weigh-ins there. What it reads is treated like anything else you log (kept on your iPhone and in your account). Health data is never used for advertising or shared with anyone else. You can change what Setpoint may read or save at any time in the Health app.

5. Camera and photos. Used only when you choose to: scan a barcode, photograph a nutrition label or your plate, or add a progress photo. Barcodes, labels and food photos are read on your iPhone by Apple's built-in tools. A food photo isn't stored. Progress photos are kept on your iPhone and in your account.

6. Searching by voice. You can tap the microphone and say a food to search for it. To turn your voice into text, Setpoint uses Apple's speech recognition, which runs on your iPhone where Apple supports it for your language; otherwise Apple processes the recording under its own privacy terms. The microphone is used only while you're speaking to the app, and Setpoint doesn't keep the recording.

7. Recipe links. When you import a recipe from a link, the app downloads that page from its website to read the recipe, the way your browser would. That website can see your IP address; it doesn't receive your diary or account details.

8. Food lookups. When you scan a barcode that isn't in the app's own food list, the barcode number is sent to Open Food Facts, a public food database, to find the product. Like any online service, they can see your IP address. They don't receive your diary or account details.

9. On your iPhone only. Reminders, the app lock (Face ID or your passcode), widgets and the Lock Screen calories are handled by your iPhone. Face ID is checked by iOS; Setpoint never gets your face data.

Legal bases

We rely on your explicit consent for storing your health data in your account; on providing the service you asked for for your account and app features; and on our legitimate interest in keeping the app secure. This follows Egypt's Personal Data Protection Law (Law No. 151 of 2020) and, for people in the European Economic Area or the UK, the GDPR.

Where your data is stored

On your iPhone, in the app's own storage. In your account, with Google Cloud Firestore and Firebase Authentication, provided by Google, which act as our service providers (processors). Google's servers may be located outside Egypt, including in the United States and the European Union. By giving consent when you create an account or sign in, you agree to this transfer. Google protects data with encryption in transit and at rest, and our security rules let only you read or change your account's data.

The same account works in Setpoint on the web and in our Study Planner app.

Who we share it with

  • Google (Firebase Authentication and Firestore), only to run your account. If you choose Google sign-in, Google also handles that sign-in.
  • Open Food Facts, only the barcodes you look up.
  • Apple, when its speech recognition can't run on your iPhone: only what you say to the app, to turn it into text.
  • Authorities, only if the law requires it.

We never sell your data, and we don't share it with advertisers or data brokers.

How long we keep it

  • On your iPhone: until you erase it (You → Settings → Erase all data on this iPhone) or delete the app.
  • In your account: until you delete your account. Deleting it removes your synced data and photos from our database straight away; Google may keep encrypted backups for a short time before they are overwritten. What's on your iPhone stays until you erase it.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, download or delete your personal data, withdraw consent, or object to how it is used. Most of this you can do yourself in the app: edit anything you logged, download it all (You → Back up, or You → Export to a spreadsheet), and delete your account (You → Account → Delete account). For anything else, email setpointapp.support@gmail.com and we'll reply within 30 days.

If you're unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to Egypt's Personal Data Protection Center, or, in the EU/UK, to your local data protection authority.

Children

Setpoint is not meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data. Under Egyptian law anyone under 18 is a child: if you're under 18, you need a parent's or guardian's permission to create an account. If you believe a child has given us data without permission, contact us and we'll delete it.

Security

Connections are encrypted, your account's data can only be read by you, and you can lock the app with Face ID or your passcode (You → Settings → Lock with Face ID). No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password and keep your iPhone locked.

Changes

If we change this policy we'll update the date at the top and, for important changes, tell you in the app. Using Setpoint after a change means you accept the updated policy.
