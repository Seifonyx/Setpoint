Setpoint is a free food, nutrition and weight tracker that runs in your browser and installs like an app. Log meals from a food list with Egyptian and everyday foods, scan barcodes and labels, and see calories, protein, carbs, fat, fiber and every vitamin and mineral against your own targets.

Get food suggestions that fill what you still need today, plan a full day of meals for your targets, follow your weight trend, and let Setpoint work out your real energy expenditure (TDEE) from your weigh-ins. It works offline, in English and Arabic, and keeps your data on your device unless you choose to sync.
