Do we use cookies?

Setpoint itself doesn't set any cookies. We don't use advertising, analytics or tracking cookies, or any other trackers, and we don't embed third-party content such as social media buttons or videos.

Because nothing optional is stored, we don't show a cookie consent banner: the law only requires consent for storage that isn't needed to provide what you asked for.

What the app stores on your device

  • Local storage and IndexedDB: your diary, weigh-ins, settings and photos, so the app works and keeps your data between visits. Strictly necessary.
  • App cache (service worker): a copy of the app and food database, so Setpoint opens fast and works offline. Strictly necessary.
  • Sign-in: if you sign in, Firebase Authentication keeps you signed in using your browser's storage. Strictly necessary.

Other sites

If you choose "Continue with Google", Google's sign-in page may set its own cookies on Google's domain, under Google's cookie policy.

How to clear it

In the app: Settings → Erase all data. In your browser: clear site data for setpoint-tracker.web.app. If you clear it without a backup or an account, your data on that device is gone.
