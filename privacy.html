Who we are

Setpoint is a food, nutrition and weight tracking app at setpoint-tracker.web.app. It is run by an individual based in Egypt under the name Setpoint ("we", "us"). We are the controller of the personal data described here.

Contact for anything about your data: setpointapp.support@gmail.com.

The short version

  • Setpoint works without an account. Without one, everything you enter stays on your device and we never receive it.
  • If you create an account, a copy of your data is stored in your account so you can sync and restore it. We use it only to run the app for you.
  • We don't show ads, don't use analytics or tracking, and don't sell or rent your data.
  • You can download everything (Settings → Back up & restore) and delete your account and cloud data at any time (You → Account → Delete account).

What we collect and why

1. What you enter in the app. Your profile (sex, birth year, height and, if you add it, body fat), weigh-ins, food diary, recipes and meals, water, fasting, body measurements, progress photos, notes, targets and settings. This is used to show your diary, targets, trends and insights.

Without an account this is kept only in your browser's storage on your device. With an account, a copy is also stored in your account (see "Where your data is stored").

2. Health data. Weight, food intake, body measurements and photos are health data, which the law treats as sensitive. We only store it in the cloud with your explicit consent, which you give when you sign in or create an account. You can withdraw that consent at any time by deleting your account.

3. Account details. If you create an account: your email address and an account ID. Passwords are handled by Google Firebase Authentication; we never see or store your password. If you sign in with Google, Google shares your name, email address and profile picture with the sign-in service; Setpoint keeps only your email address and account ID.

4. Food lookups. When you scan a barcode or search for packaged foods, the barcode or search words are sent to Open Food Facts, a public food database. Like any website, they can see your IP address. They don't receive your diary or account details.

5. Camera and microphone. Used only when you choose to scan a barcode or label, take a progress photo or speak a food. Barcode and label reading happen on your device. Progress photos are stored on your device and, if you have an account, in your account. Voice input uses your browser's own speech recognition; on some browsers (for example Chrome and Safari) the browser maker (Google or Apple) processes the audio to turn it into text, under their own privacy terms.

6. Technical data. Our hosting provider (Google Firebase Hosting) automatically records standard request information such as IP address, browser type and time of request, to deliver the app and keep it secure. The label-reading engine (Tesseract.js) is downloaded from the jsDelivr network the first time you scan a label, so jsDelivr also sees your IP address then.

Legal bases

We rely on your explicit consent for storing your health data in your account; on providing the service you asked for for your account and app features; and on our legitimate interest in keeping the app secure. This follows Egypt's Personal Data Protection Law (Law No. 151 of 2020) and, for people in the European Economic Area or the UK, the GDPR.

Where your data is stored

Account data is stored with Google Cloud Firestore and Firebase Authentication, provided by Google, which act as our service providers (processors). Google's servers may be located outside Egypt, including in the United States and the European Union. By giving consent at sign-in you agree to this transfer. Google protects data with encryption in transit and at rest, and our security rules let only you read or change your account's data.

Setpoint uses the same sign-in service as our Study Planner app, so one account works in both.

Who we share it with

  • Google (Firebase Authentication, Firestore and Hosting), only to run the app.
  • Open Food Facts, only the barcodes and search words you look up.
  • Authorities, only if the law requires it.

We never sell your data, and we don't share it with advertisers or data brokers.

How long we keep it

  • Data on your device: until you delete it (Settings → Erase all data) or clear your browser's data.
  • Account data: until you delete your account. Deleting it removes your synced data and photos from our database straight away; Google may keep encrypted backups for a short time before they are overwritten.
  • Hosting logs: kept by Google for a limited period under its own retention rules.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, download or delete your personal data, withdraw consent, or object to how it is used. Most of this you can do yourself in the app: edit anything you logged, download it all (Settings → Back up & restore), and delete your account (You → Account → Delete account). For anything else, email setpointapp.support@gmail.com and we'll reply within 30 days.

If you're unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to Egypt's Personal Data Protection Center, or, in the EU/UK, to your local data protection authority.

Children

Setpoint is not meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data. Under Egyptian law anyone under 18 is a child: if you're under 18, you need a parent's or guardian's permission to create an account. If you believe a child has given us data without permission, contact us and we'll delete it.

Security

Connections are encrypted (HTTPS), account data can only be read by its owner, and you can add an app lock (Face ID or your passcode) in Settings. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password and keep your device locked.

Changes

If we change this policy we'll update the date at the top and, for important changes, tell you in the app. Using Setpoint after a change means you accept the updated policy.
